# How to put this online (GitHub Pages, no coding)

1. Go to https://github.com/new
2. Repository name: `grokbutt-halloween-pfp`. Choose **Public**, then **Create repository**.
3. On the new repo page, click **uploading an existing file**.
4. Unzip `grokbutt-halloween-pfp-upload.zip` and drag **everything inside it** into the upload box: index.html, app.js, art.js, meta.js, style.css, card.png, favicon.png, apple-touch-icon.png, favicon.ico, the `assets` folder, README.md and .nojekyll.
   (Upload the files themselves, not the folder that contains them.)
5. Click **Commit changes**.
6. Open **Settings → Pages**. Under "Branch", pick **main** and **/(root)**, then click **Save**.
7. Wait 1–2 minutes. The site will be at https://krog4206941.github.io/grokbutt-halloween-pfp/
