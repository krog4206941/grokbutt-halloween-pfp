# GROKBUTT Halloween PFP Maker 🎃

**Trick or Crop?** Dress up GrokButt in 28 Halloween costumes, or haunt your own PFP with 40 stickers.
It's free and runs 100% in your browser, so your photo never leaves your device.

Live: https://krog4206941.github.io/grokbutt-halloween-pfp/

- **Dress up GrokButt:** 28 costume presets, including the rivals Jollybob, Dotz Crew and Clawdeth. Mix and match hats, eyes, mouths, faces, outfits, capes, props, skins and poses. Pick from 17 backgrounds, 15 frames with colour pickers, and 11 effects (fog, glow, moonlight and more). Add your @handle and hit randomize.
- **Haunt my PFP:** upload a photo, then drag, pinch, scale and rotate stickers on top (touch works too). Finish with frames and a spooky grade.
- **Export:** a 1000×1000 PNG, plus a circle-crop preview.

Made by Krog & Grok Bot
